# PHASE 14 — Voice, proactive intelligence and advanced JARVIS capabilities

## Objective

Introduce natural voice interaction and advanced personal assistance.

### Step 14.1 — Speech recognition

Integrate an offline speech recognition system such as Whisper.

Implement:

- Microphone selection.
- Audio capture.
- Voice activity detection.
- Speech transcription.
- Language identification.
- Transcription correction.

### Step 14.2 — Text-to-speech

Integrate a local TTS engine such as Piper.

Implement:

- Voice selection.
- Speech generation.
- Audio playback.
- Speech interruption.
- Voice settings.

### Step 14.3 — Real-time voice conversations

Implement:

- Continuous listening when explicitly enabled.
- Conversation turn detection.
- Low-latency responses.
- Interruptions.
- Microphone permissions.
- Listening indicators.

### Step 14.4 — Personal automation

Support:

- Reminders.
- Scheduled tasks.
- Calendar integration.
- Daily summaries.
- Project follow-ups.
- Notifications.

### Step 14.5 — Proactive intelligence

Implement configurable background processes that identify relevant events and suggest appropriate actions.

Do not allow proactive intelligence to silently perform sensitive operations.

**Completion criteria:** The application supports voice interaction and useful proactive assistance.
